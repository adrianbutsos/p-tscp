# TSCP Creative Planning

Use this skill after strategy slots have been selected for a TSCP calendar.

Read `AGENTS.md`, the active TSCP configuration, the benchmark evidence, and the strategy slots. For every slot, provide an original English idea, a concise CTA, and a practical skeleton for `individual_post`, `reel_vertical_video`, or `carousel`. Tailor the structure to the platform, keep it useful to volunteer designers, and do not copy external wording or claim unverified TSCP results. Preserve IDs and benchmark references.
